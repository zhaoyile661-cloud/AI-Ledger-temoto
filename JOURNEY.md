# My Journey

## Every Product Starts with a Problem

AI Ledger did not begin as a business idea.

It began with a simple question:

**Why is bookkeeping still so complicated in 2026?**

Every bookkeeping app I tried required users to manually type merchant names, amounts, categories, dates and notes.

The process was repetitive.

Many people start keeping accounts with enthusiasm but give up after only a few days because recording every expense becomes another daily task.

I believed AI could completely change that experience.

Instead of asking people to adapt to software, software should adapt to people.

That became the starting point of AI Ledger.

---

## A Different Vision

The goal was never to create another bookkeeping application.

The goal was to create an experience where bookkeeping almost disappears.

Imagine this:

You finish your meal.

You take one photo of the receipt.

Within seconds, AI extracts the merchant, date, amount and purchased items, categorizes everything automatically, and stores the information in your expense history.

No manual typing.

No complicated forms.

No unnecessary clicks.

Bookkeeping should feel effortless.

That vision has guided every decision during development.

---

## Building with AI

This project was built during the rise of AI-assisted software development.

Using Lovable allowed ideas to become working software much faster than traditional development.

However, AI did not build the product by itself.

Every screen, every workflow, every interaction and every decision still required careful thinking, continuous testing and countless revisions.

Writing code is only a small part of building software.

Creating a product that feels simple, reliable and enjoyable is much harder.

---

## Hundreds of Small Improvements

Most people only see the finished product.

They never see what happened behind the scenes.

During development, nearly every part of the application was redesigned, optimized or rebuilt.

Some examples include:

- Redesigning the landing page several times.
- Rebuilding the login experience.
- Optimizing automatic login.
- Improving application startup performance.
- Improving button responsiveness.
- Increasing OCR recognition accuracy.
- Reducing OCR processing time.
- Improving AI conversations.
- Supporting multiple languages.
- Integrating cloud synchronization.
- Integrating Paddle subscriptions.
- Writing privacy policies, refund policies and terms of service.
- Preparing the application for real users.

Many features were implemented more than once before reaching the current version.

Every improvement, no matter how small, made the product better.

---

## Learning Through Challenges

Building AI Ledger was also a learning journey.

One of the biggest challenges was preparing the product for payment processing.

The first Paddle review did not pass.

Instead of giving up, I carefully reviewed every suggestion.

The product description became clearer.

The landing page became more trustworthy.

The wording was updated from **AI Financial Assistant** to **AI Bookkeeping Assistant** to better reflect the actual purpose of the application.

Policies were rewritten.

User experience was improved.

Every review helped make the product more professional.

Looking back, those revisions were valuable, not obstacles.
